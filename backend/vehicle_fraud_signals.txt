VEHICLE INSURANCE FRAUD SIGNALS

1.  CLAIM AMOUNT VS DAMAGE  (DO IT/POSSIBLE)

-   Low damage but high claim amount → Suspicious
-   Minor scratch but large repair cost → Fraud risk
-   Total loss claim but damage not severe → Fraud

2.  POLICE REPORT ISSUES  (IF ATTRIBUTE PRESENT DO IT/POSSIBLE)

-   No police report for major accident → Suspicious
-   Fake or inconsistent police report details → Fraud
-   Delay in filing report → Risk signal

3.  CLAIM FREQUENCY (MULTIPLE CLAIMS)  (DO IT/POSSIBLE)

-   Too many claims in short time (>3/year) → High risk
-   Multiple claims before policy expiry → Suspicious
-   Claims immediately after policy activation → Fraud pattern
-   Repeated claims for similar damage → Strong fraud signal

4.  USER HISTORY PATTERNS  (TRY IF POSSIBLE)

-   Previous fraud flags → High risk
-   Increasing claim amounts over time → Suspicious
-   Same user in multiple suspicious claims → Fraud risk
-   Claims across different locations quickly → Impossible scenario

5.  DAMAGE CONSISTENCY CHECK  (TRY IF POSSIBLE)

-   Damage doesn’t match accident → Fraud
-   Same damage claimed multiple times → Duplicate
-   Old damage claimed as new → Fraud

6.  DOCUMENTATION ISSUES  (IF ATTRIBUTE PRESENT DO IT/POSSIBLE PUT IT IN FRONTEND PART ONLY TRY)

-   Missing documents → Suspicious
-   Duplicate invoices → Fraud
-   Tampered bills → Fraud

7. VEHICLE-RELATED SIGNALS

-   Same vehicle total loss multiple times → Fraud
-   Old vehicle high claim → Suspicious

8. CLAIM AMOUNT OUTLIERS

-   Much higher than average → Fraud
-   Sudden spike vs history → Suspicious

9. POLICY MISUSE (DO IT/POSSIBLE)

-   Claim right after purchase → Fraud
-   Upgrade before claim → Suspicious
-   Expiry manipulation → Fraud


10. DUPLICATE CLAIMS  (DO IT/POSSIBLE)

-   Same claim repeated → Fraud
-   Same damage across policies → Fraud

11. COMBINED RISK   (DO IT/POSSIBLE)

-   High amount + no report + many claims → HIGH FRAUD
-   Low damage + high amount + repeats → FRAUD
-   Clean history + proper docs → LEGIT

AI reasoning
